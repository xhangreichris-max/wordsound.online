How to use this list

Give the list to students preparing oral presentations and ask them to locate the primary stress in each word before reading the word in a sentence. The length forces deliberate pacing. Clinicians can use five-syllable items for sustained phonation or for practicing even stress across a longer sequence. Parents helping with advanced spelling or vocabulary can clap five beats while the child writes or spells aloud. Poets drafting lines that need a full five-beat foot find ready candidates already measured.

What makes a word 5-syllable

Five vowel sounds define the category. The dictionary records five sonority peaks; the written form may contain additional silent letters or digraphs that do not create extra beats.

Words people commonly miscount

Words that invite miscounts include “university,” sometimes reduced in rapid speech yet listed with five nuclei (/ˌjuː.nɪˈvɜr.sɪ.ti/). “Opportunity” keeps five in the citation form (/ˌɑp.ərˈtuː.nɪ.ti/) even when speakers compress it. “Communication” is another long form that remains five (/kəˌmjuː.nɪˈkeɪ.ʃən/). “Imagination” follows the same pattern (/ɪˌmædʒ.ɪˈneɪ.ʃən/). The list therefore supplies both practice material and a check against common spoken reductions.
